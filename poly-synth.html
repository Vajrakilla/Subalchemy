4-Voice Polyphonic Analog Synthesizer

KiCad | 0402 SMD | LTspice | Analog Design

Overview & Assembly

To design, route, and assemble a fully analog 4-voice polyphonic synthesizer from scratch. This project required robust printed circuit board design to handle sensitive analog audio signals without cross-talk.

The Tech Stack

  • PCB Design: KiCad (4-Layer Board, separating signal, ground, and power planes)
  • Simulation: LTspice for verifying analog oscillator stability.
  • Assembly: Hand-soldered using a microscope, working with components down to 0402 surface-mount (SMD) sizes.
  • Core Components: Discrete op-amps, analog oscillators, and precision resistors/capacitors.
